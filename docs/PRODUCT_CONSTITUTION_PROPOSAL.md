# UNITY Product Constitution v1 — PROPOSAL, awaiting owner review

**Status: DRAFT.** This is a non-sensitive product design reference in the public source repository, not a final approved constitution, a grant to connect accounts, or permission to activate autonomous actions.

## What UNITY is
A user-controlled intelligence and orchestration layer connecting the person's chosen apps, projects, data, work and AI systems. It is extensible to any user persona while initial functionality is tested with consenting pilot users. UNITY coordinates specialist applications; it does not aim to clone them.

## Mission
Give every person one trusted intelligence that understands their authorized digital world, helps them make informed decisions and coordinates work while keeping them in control.

## Core experience
- Home is an approachable entry point and contextual briefing, never a massive technical dashboard.
- Projects organize objectives across apps rather than treating repositories as projects.
- Conversations provide interaction; Knowledge keeps source-backed, user-owned understanding.
- Tasks, Automations, Integrations and administration are purposeful rooms, disclosed when relevant.
- The user may speak naturally; UNITY proposes and explains routing, and the user can override it.
- Underlying systems are not required to appear as everyday navigation.
- A relationship graph is embedded under Knowledge and available for optional inspection, not mandatory graph maintenance.

## Personal, operational, decision and self intelligence
1. **Personal:** Develop a user-controlled profile from explicit preferences, approved patterns and relevant project context. Distinguish observation, inference and verified fact. Do not silently treat inferred personal traits as authoritative.
2. **Operational:** Merge authorized, source-cited information across applications; retain project/account isolation and synchronization receipts. A missing feed cannot be reported as no activity.
3. **Decision:** Assess feasibility, alternatives, tradeoffs and likely consequences. Offer material improvements without arguing about every simple instruction; respect the user's informed choice unless hard safety or permission limits apply.
4. **Self:** Observe UNITY's own measured health, diagnose failures, reproduce them, propose/implement bounded fixes in isolated branches and independently verify outcomes. No unconstrained self-modification, secret exfiltration, silent production changes or unsupported "bulletproof" claims.

## Privacy and ownership
- User data and connected-account scopes remain under user control; provider-independent export is a requirement.
- Encryption in transit, at rest and in protected backups is required for production private data, with secret keys separate from the public repository.
- Human operator access denied by default; scoped temporary audited access only if expressly permitted and technically necessary.
- Minimum necessary data shared with external AI; retrieved documents and webpages never override permissions.
- Connected information does not automatically become trusted permanent memory.
- No claim of end-to-end encryption or inaccessible operator plaintext until the specific architecture has been implemented and independently tested.

## Self-operations lifecycle
Observe -> Reproduce -> Diagnose -> Propose -> Permission check -> Repair in isolation -> Test -> Independent verify -> Approval/deploy according to predefined policy -> Record outcome -> Monitor regression. Incidents retain environment, evidence, cause, intervention, verification and known limitations. Repeated failures are independently diagnosed rather than blindly replayed.

## Feature challenge gate
Before substantial work: does it reduce context switching and mental load, respect ownership/privacy, fit an existing room or genuinely warrant a new one, and remain testable/reversible? If a suggestion adds complexity without corresponding benefit, recommend a simpler alternative. Do not confuse conceptual feasibility with access to authorized APIs or sustainable economics.

## Unsettled decisions for explicit owner approval
- Exactly which areas remain permanent primary navigation after observation.
- Whether and when particular automations may perform external writes without per-action approval.
- Default retention period and scope of personal profile inference.
- What, if any, support-access pathway exists for sensitive encrypted content.
- When to graduate from personal alpha to general user onboarding and which measurable trial criteria apply.

No deployment, account connection, or activation follows from accepting this draft alone.
